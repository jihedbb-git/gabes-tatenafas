School status

School health protocol

safe Normal conditions
  • Outdoor activities allowed.
  • Standard supervision, keep students hydrated.
  • Check asthmatic children before sports.
warning Watch
  • Limit intense outdoor physical activities.
  • Shorter recess, keep asthmatic children indoors.
  • Notify parents through the communication notebook.
  • Limited ventilation, only slightly open windows.
danger / critical Suspension
  • Immediate suspension of outdoor activities and sports.
  • Windows and doors closed, minimal ventilation.
  • Urgent notification to parents (SMS + notebook).
  • Reinforced medical monitoring for at-risk children.
  • If symptoms persist: call 190 (SAMU).
