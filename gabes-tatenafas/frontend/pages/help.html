About Nafass, color codes, user roles, and shortcuts.

About

Nafass is an intelligent environmental and health alert system for the city of Gabès.

The application helps authorities, schools and citizens monitor air quality, report incidents, track symptoms and generate reports.

Color Codes

Safe — normal situation Warning — caution Danger — high risk Critical — health emergency

Roles

  • Citizen — view air quality, report issues, declare symptoms, chat with Nafass.
  • Health Authority — dashboard, statistics, reports, decisions.
  • School Director — school mode, guidelines, parent notifications.
  • Administrator — global data and zone management.

Shortcuts

  • Dashboard — quick daily overview.
  • Map — click a zone to see details.
  • Reports — describe an incident.
  • Reports — export to PDF (Ctrl + P).
